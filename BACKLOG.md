# FaunaSphere — v5 backlog
These are items from VIDEO_NOTES_1/2/3 that we deferred so v4 could ship. Details marked (guess) are our own interpretation.

## Genetics
- A dominant/recessive genome, with rare mutations, as in Esteban's lecture.
- The Gene Test Kit reveals the full Genome screen: Coat, Cooling, Ear, Eye, Pattern, Primary/Secondary Color, Species, Tail.
- Cross-species eggs from a recessive species gene. v4 has a simple chance-based version.
- Eggs can carry both Heatproof and Coldproof. Add Coldproof Gene Food.
- The egg shakes in the Nest and is clicked to hatch. v4 uses a timer and a Hatch button.
- Profile card showing Level and Generation.

## Dens and comfort
- Common Den needs 1 item each for Shelter, Comfort, Beauty and Amusement.
- Items have smiley values; den quality gives a buff (bad 10% up to 100% at 20 smilies).
- Energy buff: "gaining energy 40% faster, losing 40% slower".

## Crafting
- Combining Basin: 9 Leaf + 9 Patty makes a Pullyleaf tree; 10 Duskroot makes Arctic Mulch.
- Fuzzy Stump, Paleo Stump (Completed Skeleton: 1 Skull, 1 Body, 2 Legs, 2 Arms), and Differential Stump (10 Amber makes Amber Compound).
- Food trees: grow for 15 min, multiple harvests.
- Food flavours, for example "<name> thinks: Flavors: Bland". Gruffle/Muffle restore 350; Pullyleaf restores 400 and gives 3 smilies.
